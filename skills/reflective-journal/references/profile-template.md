# Journal profile template

```markdown

## Who I am
- Name or nickname to call me:
- Where I live:
- What I do:

## Language and style
- Language / regional variety (e.g. neutral Spanish without voseo, Brazilian Portuguese, British English):
- Things about the tone I like or dislike:
- How long I like replies to be:

## What I'm focusing on now
- (goals, habits, current projects)

## Goals already reached (don't treat them as pending)
-

## People and places that come up often
- Name — who they are to me

## How I write my entries
- (e.g. I start with time, date and place; I use the word "aside" for questions outside the journal)

## Things I'd rather you not bring up unless I do
```
